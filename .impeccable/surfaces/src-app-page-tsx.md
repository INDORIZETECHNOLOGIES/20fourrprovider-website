# Provider landing page (/)

Scope: public marketing page for supply-side recruitment. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: security agency owners first; individual guards, bouncers, armed guards and PSOs second.
Action: register (agency or individual). Proof: the product itself (real rate card shape, real payout arithmetic, real document list), live cities from GET /public/cities, real photos when the owner supplies them (slots ready, none invented).
Constraints: no invented numbers, customers or testimonials; contracts are switched off, so never claimed; TCS 0.5% all providers, TDS 0.1% on gross incl. GST (individuals with PAN relieved under Rs 5L/yr); payout released at duty end + invoice upload, to a Razorpay-verified payout account.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One question decides the page: agency or on your own. Refuses the category's slogan hero with a floating app mock, icon-tile card grids and a numbered 7-step timeline.

OWN-WORLD: The app's credential register at poster scale: deep navy #1b2a4a field, brass #a9782e as the one active colour, cool paper #f5f6f8, Roboto Slab set large, Public Sans body, hairline-ruled documents (rate card, settlement statement, document checklist) as the only illustration. No gradients, no icon tiles, no rounded card stacks.

STORY: The visitor learns in one line what 20fourr is, picks their door, sees exactly how pricing, teams, verification and payout work for their kind of business with real arithmetic, and registers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy field. One-line statement across the top (slab, ~40px). Below it two tall doors: agency (7/12, first, brass rule on top) and individual (5/12). Each door: a large slab title, three plain facts, a ruled artefact (agency: staff-calendar strip booked/declared; individual: credential line with rate), its own register button. The chosen door is marked and the body below swaps with it (#agency / #individual).

FORM: Two doors, ranked 6 of 7 on my list; seed b3cadfe7.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
